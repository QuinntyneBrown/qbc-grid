# Code of conduct

We want qbc-grid to be a welcoming place to learn, report problems, and improve software.
This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work. Assume good intent while remaining
  open to correction and respecting the impact of your words.
- Welcome newcomers, credit others' work, and respect privacy.
- Keep discussion relevant and accept reasonable moderation decisions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to [Quinntyne Brown](mailto:quinntynebrown@gmail.com). Include
links or context that help explain what happened. Reports will be handled with discretion;
information may be shared when necessary to investigate or protect participants.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, request changes in behavior, restrict
participation temporarily, or ban a participant, depending on severity and recurrence.
Those affected may request reconsideration through the same private contact. This is a
small maintainer-run project with no separate appeals committee.
